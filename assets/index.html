About Me

My Background

My name is Mark Keneth B. Pederio. I am 18 years old and a student pursuing my growth in technology and creative work. I completed my high school education at Naga College Foundation. I enjoy video clipping, editing, poster making, and exploring new digital tools.


Clipping can be challenging, especially when starting with raw footage and finding the right moments, timing, and flow. Those challenges motivate me to practice, improve, and learn from each output.


I am hardworking, trustworthy, patient, and open to learning. I am also interested in making my own personal AI and discovering more about technology.
